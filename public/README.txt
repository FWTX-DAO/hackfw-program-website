HACKFW MARKETING ASSETS

Public assets for https://hack.fwtx.city

USAGE

- Confirm current copy, dates, and event details at https://hack.fwtx.city before publishing.
- Send builders to https://hackfw.devpost.com for registration, rules, and submissions.
- Use AVIF for web, PNG/WebP for general digital use, and SVG for scalable logos.
- Keep logos proportional and unchanged. Do not crop, stretch, recolor, or add effects.
- Confirm sponsor names, placement, and approval before publishing partner logos.
- Contact community@fwtx.city with asset or approval questions.
